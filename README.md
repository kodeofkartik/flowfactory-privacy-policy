# FlowFactory Privacy Policy

This repository hosts the privacy policy for the **FlowFactory: Flow AI Automation** Chrome extension.

📄 **Read the policy:** [`index.md`](index.md) — or view the published page once GitHub Pages is enabled.

FlowFactory keeps your prompts and generated media on your device and on Google Flow itself. The only data sent to a third party is your Pro license key (to Gumroad, to verify your purchase). No analytics, no tracking, no selling of data.
